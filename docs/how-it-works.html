How a poster becomes a playlist

The app turns a festival poster into a playlist. The Analyze route reads the artist names once and ranks them by how big they are on the poster. The Track search route then uses that rank to decide how many songs each artist gets. Nothing is stored on the server. A draft in the browser carries the artists and songs from page to page.

03 · Bigger names get more songs

By default, a headliner gets 10 songs and an undercard act gets 1. Vision gives no tier, so every artist it finds gets 3. On Review artists the listener can replace these counts.

How to readEach bar is one category; its length is songs per artist, tier-based mode, from zero. The accent marks Headliner, the one the text is about. Select a bar to see the others as differences from it.

  1. 1One headliner brings as many songs as ten undercard acts.

What this doesn't showA track mode then picks which songs: popular, balanced or deep cuts. Each mode shuffles its pool.
