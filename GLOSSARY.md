# Music Posters

Turns a festival lineup into a playlist on a music platform. A lineup comes from a
poster image or from a list the user types.

## Lineups

**Poster Lineup**:
A lineup extracted from an uploaded poster image, with each artist ranked into a tier.
_Avoid_: Poster playlist, image lineup

**Manual Lineup**:
A lineup the user types, one artist per line, with no tiers.
_Avoid_: Text list, curated list, manual list

**Tier**:
An artist's billing level on a poster: headliner, sub-headliner, mid-tier, or undercard.
_Avoid_: Weight, rank, level

## Live sets

**Qualifying Setlist**:
A setlist from a show that has already happened and has enough performed songs to be a real set, not a promo or TV slot.
_Avoid_: Valid setlist, full setlist

**On Tour**:
The state of an artist whose newest Qualifying Setlist is recent.
_Avoid_: Active, touring

**Typical Set**:
The songs an On Tour artist plays at most recent shows, in their usual order.
_Avoid_: Tour setlist, live set, average setlist

**Setlist Scope**:
The artists that the "Prefer tour setlists" toggle covers: the headliners of a Poster Lineup, or every artist of a Manual Lineup.
_Avoid_: Setlist artists, eligible artists

**Setlist Fallback**:
The outcome for an artist who gets no Typical Set and whose tracks come from the regular selection mode.
_Avoid_: Default, no-setlist case
